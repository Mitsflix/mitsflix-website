Questions

How much does it cost?

Reseller pricing depends on how many credits you buy. We send the current price list when you apply.

How do I set my prices?

That’s up to you. You buy credits from us and charge your customers whatever you like.

Can I offer free trials?

Yes. Your panel can create short trial accounts so customers can test before they buy.

How many screens does each account get?

Each account plays on one screen at a time.

What do my customers use to watch?

Mitsflix Player on Fire TV, Google TV, Android and Windows, TiviMax on Apple devices, or any app that supports Xtream Codes. Point them to our setup guides.
