Questions

How many screens can I watch on?

One at a time. You can install the app on as many devices as you like, but only one can play at once.

Which device should I buy?

A Google TV Streamer 4K or a Fire TV Stick 4K Max. Avoid Fire TV Sticks with Select or HD in the name, they aren’t powerful enough.

How do I pay?

After you sign up we email you the payment options. Your login is sent as soon as payment comes through.

A channel isn’t working. What do I do?

First try one of the backup server addresses from your welcome email. If it still won’t play, report it in our Discord and we’ll look into it.

It keeps buffering.

Follow the buffering fixes. Switching to a backup server address or setting Cloudflare DNS on your modem fixes most cases.

Can I use my own IPTV app?

Yes. Any app that supports Xtream Codes works. Use the server, username and password from your welcome email.

Can I ask for a movie or show to be added?

Yes. Use the Make a request channel in our Discord.
