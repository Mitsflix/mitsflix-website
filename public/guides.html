Fire TV, Google TV & Android TV

Install Downloader

  1. Go to Search. On Google TV, open the Play Store.
  2. Type Downloader.
  3. Install the orange Downloader app.

Allow unknown apps

  1. Fire TV: Settings, My Fire TV, Developer Options, Install Unknown Apps, then turn Downloader on.
  2. Google TV: Settings, Apps, Security & restrictions, Unknown sources, then turn Downloader on.

No Developer Options on your Fire TV? Go to Settings, My Fire TV, About, click Fire TV Stick seven times, then go back.

Install Mitsflix Player

  1. Open Downloader.
  2. Enter code 6284643 and press Go.
  3. Press Install, then Open.

Add your login

  1. Open the app and choose Add playlist, then Xtream Codes.
  2. Server: http://cf.126762.xyz
  3. Username and Password: the login we emailed you.
  4. Save. Live TV, movies, series and the TV guide load by themselves.

Turned your VPN off? Switch back to a standard address, or the app won’t connect.

Buffering or not loading

  1. Try another server address from the list above.
  2. Restart the app, your device and your modem.
  3. Use Cloudflare DNS on your modem: primary 1.1.1.1, secondary 1.0.0.1. Turning off IPv6 in the modem can help too.
  4. Try a VPN connected to a US or European server, not one near Australia, and use a VPN-only address.
