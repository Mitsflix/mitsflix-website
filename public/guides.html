Fire TV, Google TV & Android TV

Install Downloader

  1. Go to Search. On Google TV, open the Play Store.
  2. Type Downloader.
  3. Install the orange Downloader app.

Allow unknown apps

  1. Fire TV: Settings, My Fire TV, Developer Options, Install Unknown Apps, then turn Downloader on.
  2. Google TV: Settings, Apps, Security & restrictions, Unknown sources, then turn Downloader on.

No Developer Options on your Fire TV? Go to Settings, My Fire TV, About, click Fire TV Stick seven times, then go back.

Install Mitsflix Player

  1. Open Downloader.
  2. Enter code 6284643 and press Go.
  3. Press Install, then Open.

Add your login

  1. Open the app and choose Add playlist, then Xtream Codes.
  2. Server, Username and Password: copy them exactly from the welcome email we sent you.
  3. Save. Live TV, movies and series load by themselves.

Windows, Mac & Linux

Windows 10 or 11

  1. Download Mitsflix Player for Windows and open the file.
  2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer. Mitsflix Player opens when it’s done.

Mac

  1. Download Mitsflix Player for Mac and open the file.
  2. Drag Mitsflix Player into the Applications folder.
  3. Open Mitsflix Player from Applications. macOS says it can’t check the app: choose Done.
  4. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and choose Open Anyway next to Mitsflix Player, then confirm.

You only need to approve it once. On older macOS versions, right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

Linux

  1. On Ubuntu, Debian or Mint, download the .deb and open it to install.
  2. On other systems, download the AppImage, make it executable (right-click, Properties, Permissions), then open it.

Chromebook

This works on Chromebooks with an Intel or AMD processor. It uses the Chromebook’s built-in Linux.

  1. Open Settings, then About ChromeOS, Developers, and turn on Linux development environment. Follow the steps (the defaults are fine).
  2. Download the .deb file.
  3. Open the Files app, double-click the file and choose Install.
  4. Open Mitsflix Player from the launcher. It’s in the Linux apps folder.

Steam Deck

  1. Press the Steam button, then Power, then Switch to Desktop.
  2. Open the web browser and download the AppImage.
  3. In the Downloads folder, right-click the file, choose Properties, then Permissions, and tick Is executable.
  4. To use it in Gaming Mode: open Steam, then Games, Add a Non-Steam Game, choose the file and press Add.
  5. Go back to Gaming Mode. Mitsflix Player is in your library under Non-Steam.

Buffering or not loading

  1. Try another server address. Your welcome email has backups, or ask us for one.
  2. Restart the app, your device and your modem.
  3. Use Cloudflare DNS on your modem: primary 1.1.1.1, secondary 1.0.0.1. Turning off IPv6 in the modem can help too.
  4. Try a VPN connected to a US or European server, not one near Australia. Ask us for the VPN server address to use with it.
